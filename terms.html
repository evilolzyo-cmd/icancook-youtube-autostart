Terms of use

Personal use

ICANCOOK YouTube Autostart is a personal desktop utility for the ICANCOOK account owner. This website provides information about the utility; it does not offer public accounts, subscriptions, payments or a hosted broadcast service.

Authorized actions

The owner decides whether to connect a Google account and enable the local helper. When enabled, the helper may prepare and start a public YouTube broadcast on a new OBS Twitch streaming session. The owner remains in control of the broadcast content and account settings.

The owner can stop using the utility by disabling its Windows startup task and revoking its Google access. The local utility does not replace OBS, Twitch, TikTok or YouTube.

Platform terms

Use of YouTube remains subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google account access remains subject to Google's applicable terms and policies. Platform availability, permission changes and network problems can affect automatic startup.
